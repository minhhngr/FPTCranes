### Mode
[Mode = Highest frequent value]

A mode is most frequent value

- 1 > Uni-modal
- 2 modes - Bimodal
- 3 modes - Trimodal
- More than one mode - multi-modal

### Mean

Mean is a frequently used mesaure of central tendency and generally considered the best
- Sometime situations where either media or mode

Median is the preferred of central tendency
- A few extreme scores (outliers) in the distribution of the data 
- Some missing or undetermined values
- Open ended distribution
- Measured on an ordinal scale



Using

Mean
- Summterical Data
- No Outliers
- Avg value
Ex: Heights, Test Scores

Median
- Skewed data
- With Outliers
- Middle Value
Ex: House Prices, Salaries

Mode
- Categoical Data
- Most Frequent
Ex: Favorite Colors, Shoe Sizes, Customer Preferences
